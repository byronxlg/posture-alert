# Brag Plan: posture-alert

## What is this app?
A webcam posture monitor that chimes when you slouch. Vite + React, MediaPipe Pose Landmarker in
the browser, a static site on GitHub Pages at https://byronxlg.com/posture-alert/.

## The angle
Let the real app make the case: the landing page's own headline, then ten seconds of the app
running its sample video, flipping between "Upright" and "Slouching" as the man in the clip
drops his head and sits back up. Then three plain facts from the README and the wordmark.

## Hook (first 3 s)
The landing headline, verbatim: "A nudge when you start to slouch." with the plumb-line figure.

## Key moments
- Real footage (Playwright recording of `?sample` against `vite preview`): skeleton over the
  video, the status heading switching Upright / Slouching, the session bar filling green and red.
- Facts: "The video never leaves your device." / "Sit up straight for three seconds: that is
  your baseline." / "83% accurate on 24 labelled clips, with no false alerts."

## Outro / punchline
Plumb mark + "Posture Alert", "A webcam posture monitor that chimes when you slouch.",
byronxlg.com/posture-alert/.

## Tone
- Preset: polished. Long holds, one push-in on the footage.

## Format: landscape - 1920x1080
## Duration: 20s

## Visual identity (src/app/styles.css)
- mist #dde5e3, paper #f4f7f6, ink #173036, muted #587075, good #1f8a70, bad #d1344b, stage #10242a
- Display: Bricolage Grotesque; body: Atkinson Hyperlegible (Google Fonts, as the site loads them)

## Audio direction
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10 (the same 20 s trim and RMS data as solar-system),
  volume 0.30. Cues used: 3.55 footage lands, 13.11 facts, 16.93 wordmark.
- Audio-reactive: RMS breathes a soft ink vignette only.
- SFX: soft impact under the footage landing, bong on the wordmark.

## Storyboard
1. Hook 0.0-3.2: figure and headline rise 0.2, hold to 2.85, fade.
2. The app 3.2-13.0: footage (app.mp4 from 0.5 s) scales in 3.55, caption pill "The real app,
   running its sample video" 4.0 to 6.5; slow push-in 1.0 to 1.06 across the scene; fade 12.6.
3. Facts 13.0-16.6: lines at 13.11, 13.75, 14.4, hold to 16.25.
4. Outro 16.6-20.0: wordmark 16.93, tagline 17.47, URL 18.01.

Poster: about 8.5 s, the app on Slouching with the red skeleton.
